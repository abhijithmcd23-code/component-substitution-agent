# Capacitor substitution rules (synthetic teaching knowledge base)

- Capacitance must match the original nominal capacitance for this prototype.
- Candidate tolerance should be equal to or tighter than the original tolerance.
- Candidate voltage rating must be known and equal to or greater than the original voltage rating.
- Candidate operating temperature range must cover the original operating temperature range.
- Package must match for this prototype.
- Missing critical specifications require an INCONCLUSIVE verdict, never a guess.
- Real capacitor substitution may also require checking ESR, ripple current, dielectric, bias derating, lifetime and circuit-specific behaviour; those checks are not implemented in this prototype.
